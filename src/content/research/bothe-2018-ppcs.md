## Summary

Bőthe and colleagues (2018) developed the Problematic Pornography Consumption Scale
(PPCS), now one of the most widely used instruments for measuring problematic pornography
use in research. The scale operationalizes problematic use through six components borrowed
from an addiction model.

## Why the component structure matters

Because the PPCS embeds concepts such as "tolerance" and "withdrawal" into its items, it
does not simply measure problematic use neutrally — it partly *assumes* an addiction
framework. This is relevant to the [withdrawal](/topics/withdrawal-definition/) question
and to how the [PPU construct is defined](/topics/ppu-definition/).
