## Summary

de Alarcón and colleagues (2019) systematically reviewed the literature on online
pornography "addiction." Their central conclusion is that the field lacks a uniform
definition and that many studies are methodologically weak.

## Why it matters

This review is a corrective to confident claims about PPU. It is cited here in the
[behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/) and in
[defining PPU](/topics/ppu-definition/), and it underscores why this knowledge base
distinguishes community experience from evidence.
