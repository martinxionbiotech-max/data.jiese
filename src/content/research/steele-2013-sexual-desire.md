## Summary

Steele and colleagues (2013) measured neural responses to sexual images and found they
tracked sexual desire, not hypersexuality measures. This is an early skeptical study in
the [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).

## Why it matters

The study's small, non-clinical sample limits its strength (reflected in the "low"
evidence-strength label), but it is historically important because it challenged early
claims about a distinctive "hypersexual" neural signature.
