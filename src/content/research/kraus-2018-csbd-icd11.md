## Summary

Kraus and colleagues (2018), writing in *World Psychiatry*, set out the reasoning behind
the ICD-11's inclusion of Compulsive Sexual Behaviour Disorder and describe its proposed
diagnostic criteria. The paper is a consensus/position piece authored by experts involved
in the classification process.

## What it argues

The authors position CSBD as an impulse-control disorder, distinguishing it from the
substance-use and behavioural-addiction frameworks that were under consideration. They
acknowledge the evidence base is incomplete and emphasize the need for more research on
the condition's phenomenology, course, and treatment.

## Why it matters here

This record documents the official reasoning behind the current classification, and is a
primary source for understanding why "sexual addiction" is not an ICD-11 diagnosis. See
the [CSBD topic](/topics/csbd/) and the [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).
