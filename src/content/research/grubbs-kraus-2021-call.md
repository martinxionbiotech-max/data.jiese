## Summary

Grubbs and Kraus (2021) reviewed the psychological literature on pornography use and
argued that the field suffers from polarization and conceptual confusion. They call for
research that carefully separates use from distress and that avoids assuming an
addiction framework from the outset.

## Why it matters

This paper is a useful frame for this knowledge base's approach: distinguishing what
people report from what evidence establishes, and treating the [behavioural addiction
debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/) as genuinely open rather than settled.
