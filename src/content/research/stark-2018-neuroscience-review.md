## Summary

Stark and colleagues (2018) reviewed the behavioural neuroscience of CSBD and problematic
pornography use. The review concludes that research points toward altered reward
processing and cue reactivity in affected individuals, but emphasizes the heterogeneity
and incompleteness of the evidence.

## Why it matters

This review is a useful, cautious summary of the [neuroscience](/neuroscience/) of
compulsive sexual behaviour, and it explicitly resists overclaiming. It frames what the
brain-imaging literature can and cannot currently say.
