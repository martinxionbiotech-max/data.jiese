## Summary

Kraus, Voon, and Potenza (2016) published a widely cited review asking whether compulsive
sexual behaviour should be classified as an addiction. They reviewed neurobiological and
clinical parallels with substance-use and gambling disorders.

## Their conclusion

The authors concluded that some people's compulsive sexual behaviour shows addiction-like
features, but that the evidence was not yet strong enough to justify formal classification
as an addiction. This measured position is a useful reference point for the
[behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).
