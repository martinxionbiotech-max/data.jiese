## Summary

Voon and colleagues (2014) used functional MRI to compare brain responses to sexual cues
in people with and without compulsive sexual behaviours. The compulsive-behaviour group
showed heightened activity in regions linked to reward anticipation and craving.

## Why it matters

This is one of the foundational studies on [neural cue reactivity](/topics/cue-reactivity-neural/)
and is frequently cited in discussions of [craving](/topics/craving/) and the
[reward system](/topics/reward-system/). Its small size and cross-sectional design mean it
suggests — but does not prove — that these brain differences are causally linked to the
behaviour.
