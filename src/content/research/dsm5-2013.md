## Summary

The American Psychiatric Association's DSM-5 (2013) does **not** include "hypersexual
disorder" or "sexual addiction" as diagnoses. Proposed criteria for hypersexual disorder
were considered during the DSM-5 process but were not adopted.

## Why it matters

This is a significant data point in the [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/):
the two major classification systems differ. The ICD-11 includes [CSBD](/topics/csbd/),
while the DSM-5 does not include a comparable category. This divergence reflects genuine,
unresolved scientific disagreement.
