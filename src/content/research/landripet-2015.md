## Summary

Landripet and Štulhofer (2015) examined whether pornography use was associated with
sexual difficulties in young heterosexual men. They found little consistent association.

## Why it matters

This is one of the few studies directly testing the [PIED hypothesis](/topics/pied/) in a
general population sample. It found no reliable link, though it cannot rule out problems
in specific subgroups. Together with [Prause & Pfaus (2015)](/research/prause-pfaus-2015/),
it represents the skeptical side of the PIED question.
