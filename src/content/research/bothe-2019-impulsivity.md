## Summary

Bőthe and colleagues (2019) examined whether impulsivity or compulsivity better
characterizes problematic sexual behaviour. Across a large sample, compulsivity showed
the stronger association, challenging purely impulsivity-based explanations.

## Why it matters

This finding bears directly on how we talk about [impulse control](/topics/impulse-control/)
and [compulsive sexual behaviour](/topics/compulsive-sexual-behavior/): the experience may
be less about sudden impulsive action and more about repeated, hard-to-stop patterns.
