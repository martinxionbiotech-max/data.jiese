## Summary

Gola and colleagues (2017) scanned men seeking treatment for problematic pornography use
while they viewed cues that predicted erotic images. The key finding was heightened
ventral striatal response to the *predictive cue* rather than the image itself.

## Why it matters

This result is consistent with incentive-salience models of [dopamine](/topics/dopamine/)
and the [reward system](/topics/reward-system/): the brain response tracks anticipation
more than pleasure. However, as a small correlational study in a treatment-seeking group,
it does not establish that pornography causes these differences. It is also sometimes
read as evidence in the [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).
