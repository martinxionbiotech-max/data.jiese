## What this record covers

The World Health Organization's International Classification of Diseases, 11th Revision
(ICD-11) was adopted by the World Health Assembly in May 2019 and came into effect on
1 January 2022. It includes a diagnosis titled **Compulsive Sexual Behaviour Disorder**
(CSBD), code **6C72**, placed in the chapter on **impulse control disorders**.

## Key diagnostic features

CSBD is defined as a persistent pattern of failure to control intense, repetitive sexual
impulses or urges, resulting in repetitive sexual behaviour that causes marked distress or
significant impairment. The pattern must be present for about six months or more and must
not be better accounted for by another condition.

## What the classification deliberately avoids

The ICD-11 does not use the label "sexual addiction" and does not place CSBD among the
disorders due to substance use or addictive behaviours. This placement reflects an
ongoing scientific disagreement about how best to conceptualize the condition. For a
fuller discussion see the [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).
