## Summary

Walton and colleagues (2017) critically reviewed the concept of hypersexuality and
questioned whether it constitutes a distinct disorder. They proposed an alternative
"sexhavior cycle" model.

## Why it matters

This paper represents the skeptical side of the classification debate and is relevant to
[compulsive sexual behaviour](/topics/compulsive-sexual-behavior/) and the
[behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).
