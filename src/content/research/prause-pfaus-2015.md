## Summary

Prause and Pfaus (2015) reviewed the literature on viewing sexual stimuli and sexual
responsiveness, concluding that greater viewing was generally associated with greater
responsiveness, not erectile dysfunction. This is a key counterpoint to the
[PIED hypothesis](/topics/pied/).

## Why it matters

This review does not close the question — it reviews heterogeneous studies and does not
rule out problems in specific subgroups — but it directly challenges the strong claim that
pornography viewing reliably causes erectile dysfunction.
