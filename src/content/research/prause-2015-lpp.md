## Summary

Prause and colleagues (2015) measured an EEG signal (the late positive potential) while
people with self-reported pornography problems viewed sexual images. They found no
addiction-like amplified response, and argued the results are inconsistent with a
"porn addiction" model.

## Why it matters

This study is important for the [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/)
because it provides a skeptical data point. It has itself been criticized, and its
interpretation remains contested. It is included here to represent both sides of the
[neural cue reactivity](/topics/cue-reactivity-neural/) question honestly.
