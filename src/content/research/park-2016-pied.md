## Summary

Park and colleagues (2016) reviewed clinical reports describing young men with erectile
dysfunction attributed to internet pornography use. The paper is widely cited in
discussions of "porn-induced erectile dysfunction" (PIED).

## What it can and cannot support

The review documents that some clinicians report seeing such cases, and hypothesizes a
causal link. However, it is based on clinical reports rather than controlled studies, and
it has been criticized for selection bias. It does **not** establish that pornography
causes erectile dysfunction. See the [PIED topic](/topics/pied/) for the full,
balanced picture.
