## What craving is

Craving is an intense, often conscious desire or urge for a specific experience. In
problematic pornography use it is the strong felt pull toward pornography that can appear
suddenly, often in response to a cue.

## What research suggests

Some neuroimaging work, such as [Voon et al. (2014)](/research/voon-2014-cue-reactivity/)
and [Gola et al. (2017)](/research/gola-2017-fmri/), found heightened activity in
reward-anticipation regions during sexual cue exposure in people with compulsive sexual
behaviour. These findings are consistent with the idea that craving involves the brain's
anticipation machinery. However, the studies are small and correlational.

## Practical angle

A key self-regulation skill is recognizing craving as a temporary rise-and-fall rather
than a command to act. Craving does not require acting on. See
[urge vs. craving](/topics/urge-vs-craving/) for the distinction.
