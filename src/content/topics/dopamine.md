## What dopamine is

Dopamine is a neurotransmitter involved in motivation, reward anticipation, learning, and
movement. It is often, misleadingly, called the "pleasure chemical." In fact, research on
incentive salience indicates dopamine tracks **wanting** — anticipation and motivation —
more closely than **liking**.

## What is actually known

The idea that dopamine is central to reward anticipation is well established. In PPU,
neuroimaging findings such as [Gola et al. (2017)](/research/gola-2017-fmri/) are
consistent with this "wanting" account. However, dopamine cannot be measured directly in
everyday human contexts, and most popular claims about dopamine and pornography are
extrapolations.

## Claims to be cautious about

The common claim that pornography "depletes," "destroys," or "fries" dopamine receptors is
not supported by evidence. Dopamine is not a finite resource, and no high-quality study
establishes that pornography causes lasting, harmful dopamine changes in humans.
