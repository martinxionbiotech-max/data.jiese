## What neuroplasticity is

Neuroplasticity is the brain's lifelong capacity to reorganize and change in response to
experience and learning. It is a normal, universal property — every skill and habit is
reflected in brain change.

## The damage claim

A recurring claim in popular recovery content is that pornography "rewires" or
"permanently damages" the brain. There is **no high-quality evidence** supporting this.
Brain-imaging studies sometimes report group differences in how people with compulsive
sexual behaviour respond to sexual cues, but these are small, correlational, and do not
show permanent damage or tissue loss.

## What neuroplasticity actually implies

Neuroplasticity cuts both ways: the same capacity for change that underpins a habit also
underpins changing it. It is a reason for realistic optimism, not a warrant for exaggerated
"brain damage" claims. See [reward system](/topics/reward-system/) and
[dopamine](/topics/dopamine/).
