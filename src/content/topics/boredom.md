## What boredom is

Boredom is the aversive feeling of wanting to engage in satisfying activity but being
unable to — restless disengagement. It is commonly reported as a trigger for impulsive
and compulsive behaviours.

## Boredom as a trigger

Boredom is one of the most frequently reported triggers for pornography use in community
reports, often in the evening or when alone with a phone. Boredom proneness is associated
with problematic Internet use generally, though controlled evidence for PPU specifically
is limited.

## Practical angle

Because boredom is often about unstructured idle time, practical strategies include
reducing idle time and building engaging alternatives, and learning to notice boredom
early before it becomes an urge. See [loneliness](/topics/loneliness/) for a closely
related trigger.
