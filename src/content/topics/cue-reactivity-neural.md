## What neural cue reactivity is

Neural cue reactivity is the brain's measurable response to behaviour-associated cues,
studied with fMRI or EEG. It is the neural counterpart of the psychological phenomenon of
[cue reactivity](/topics/cue-reactivity/).

## A mixed picture

A handful of small studies report heightened neural reactivity to sexual cues in people
with compulsive sexual behaviour — for example, [Voon et al. (2014)](/research/voon-2014-cue-reactivity/)
and [Gola et al. (2017)](/research/gola-2017-fmri/). But other work, such as
[Prause et al. (2015)](/research/prause-2015-lpp/), found no addiction-like response. The
field is small, heterogeneous, and contested, with no consensus biomarker.

## What this means practically

There is no validated brain test for problematic pornography use, and these findings do
not translate into clinical tools. Practical cue-management strategies remain useful
regardless of how the neural debate resolves.
