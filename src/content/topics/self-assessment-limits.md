## What self-assessment is

Self-assessment means using questionnaires or checklists to evaluate one's own behaviour.
Such tools can prompt useful reflection, but they are **not diagnostic** and have real
scientific limitations.

## Why this site has no self-test

Validated clinical instruments, such as structured clinical interviews, are designed for
trained professionals to administer. Self-report scales like the
[PPCS](/research/bothe-2018-ppcs/) measure problematic use in research, not diagnosis, and
are influenced by factors such as
[moral incongruence](/research/grubbs-2019-moral-incongruence/). For these reasons, this
knowledge base deliberately does not offer a self-diagnosis tool.

## What to do instead

If you are concerned, discuss it with a qualified professional rather than relying on a
score. See [seeking professional help](/topics/seeking-professional-help/).
