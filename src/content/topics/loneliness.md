## What loneliness is

Loneliness is the distressing feeling of a gap between the social connection a person
wants and what they have. It is different from simply being alone — a person can be
surrounded by others and still feel lonely.

## Loneliness as a trigger

Community members very frequently name loneliness as a trigger for pornography use,
especially when isolated, at night, or feeling disconnected. Loneliness is also a
well-established risk factor for poor mental health generally, though its specific role in
PPU is less studied.

## Practical angle

If loneliness is a primary trigger, the more relevant intervention may be building genuine
social connection rather than white-knuckling urges. See [boredom](/topics/boredom/) and
[stress](/topics/stress/) for related triggers.
