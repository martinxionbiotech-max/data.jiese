## What sexual dysfunction is

Sexual dysfunction refers to difficulties in any phase of the sexual response that cause
distress — desire, arousal, erection, orgasm, or pain. It is common, multifactorial, and
often treatable.

## Its many causes

Sexual dysfunction has biological, psychological, and relational contributors. Research on
a specific causal role for pornography use is limited and mixed; most studies find weak or
inconsistent associations. Single-cause explanations — whether "it's all pornography" or
"it's never pornography" — are unreliable.

## Practical relevance

Sexual difficulties are common and treatable. A professional evaluation can identify the
contributing factors rather than assuming a single cause. See the
[PIED topic](/topics/pied/) for the specific question of pornography and erectile
dysfunction.
