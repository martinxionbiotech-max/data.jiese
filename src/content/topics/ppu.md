## What PPU is

Problematic pornography use (PPU) is pornography use that a person experiences as out of
control, distressing, or harmful. It is a descriptive term, not a formal diagnosis.

## The definitional problem

PPU is measured in many ways — self-perceived "addiction," moral incongruence, or
behaviour scales like the [PPCS](/research/bothe-2018-ppcs/). A central finding is that
self-perceived problems are only modestly related to how much pornography a person
actually uses, and that
[moral incongruence](/research/grubbs-2019-moral-incongruence/) is a strong driver of the
"addicted" self-label.

## Practical relevance

Separating distress from amount of use can clarify what the core problem is. People can
seek help on the basis of distress alone — no formal diagnosis is required. See
[defining PPU](/topics/ppu-definition/) and
[seeking professional help](/topics/seeking-professional-help/).
