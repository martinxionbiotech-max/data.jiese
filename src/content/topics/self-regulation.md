## What self-regulation is

Self-regulation is the broad capacity to monitor and direct one's thoughts, emotions, and
behaviour toward goals. It includes attention, inhibition, planning, and persisting
through setbacks.

## How it relates to PPU

Theoretical accounts such as the [I-PACE model](/research/brand-2016-ipace/) propose that
reduced executive control and self-regulation contribute to problematic Internet and
pornography use. Community members describe recovery as a gradual strengthening of
self-regulation — better noticing urges, planning ahead, and recovering faster after
setbacks.

## Practical angle

Self-regulation can be built through practice: planning, monitoring triggers, and setting
realistic goals. Factors like sleep, stress, and general wellbeing tend to support it.
Direct causal evidence specific to PPU, however, is still limited.
