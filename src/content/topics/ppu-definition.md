## The core idea

Problematic pornography use (PPU) is the descriptive term for pornography use that a
person experiences as out of control, distressing, or disruptive. It is not a formal
diagnosis: it is a research and community term.

## Why there is no single definition

Research measures PPU in different ways — through self-perceived "addiction," through
moral incongruence, or through behaviour scales such as the
[PPCS](/research/bothe-2018-ppcs/). These measures do not always flag the same people,
which is one reason the evidence base is hard to summarize. The distinction between
distress and amount of use is central to the
[moral incongruence](/research/grubbs-2019-moral-incongruence/) findings.

## What communities mean by PPU

In recovery communities, PPU is usually defined experientially: loss of control, time
lost, shame, and life disruption. That is a legitimate description of distress, but it is
not the same thing as a clinical definition.
