## When to seek help

The core reasons to seek professional help are **distress and impairment** — not a
self-applied label. If pornography use or sexual behaviour causes significant distress,
interferes with life, or co-occurs with low mood or anxiety, professional support may be
appropriate.

## How to find help

Reasonable first points of contact include a primary-care doctor, psychologist,
psychiatrist, or licensed therapist. Many related conditions have effective treatments,
even though research specific to PPU/CSBD is still developing.

## What this site does and does not do

This knowledge base provides educational information; it does **not** diagnose or treat,
and it does not offer a self-diagnosis tool (see
[limits of self-assessment](/topics/self-assessment-limits/)). The choice between self-help,
peer support, and professional care is individual.
