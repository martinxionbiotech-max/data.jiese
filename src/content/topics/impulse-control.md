## What impulse control is

Impulse control is the ability to resist an immediate urge in order to act in line with
longer-term goals. It is closely related to, but not identical with, self-regulation and
willpower.

## Its place in the classification

The ICD-11 classifies [CSBD](/topics/csbd/) under "impulse control disorders." Yet
research such as [Bőthe et al. (2019)](/research/bothe-2019-impulsivity/) suggests
compulsivity may be more relevant than impulsivity for problematic sexual behaviour. So a
pure impulse-control account is contested.

## Practical angle

Strategies that widen the gap between urge and action — delay, distraction, changing the
environment — draw on impulse-control ideas. Whether a given person's pattern is more
impulsive or compulsive may shape which approaches help. See
[self-regulation](/topics/self-regulation/).
