## What "flatline" refers to

In recovery communities, a "flatline" is a reported period during abstinence when a person
feels low or absent sexual desire, reduced erections, flat mood, or low energy. It is a
**community term**, not a medical or clinical one.

## Its clinical status

"Flatline" does not appear in the ICD-11 or DSM-5, and there is no established clinical
definition. Little systematic research has examined the phenomenon. This is important: a
reported experience can be real and still not be a medically recognized condition.

## How to interpret reports

Community descriptions of flatlines are anecdotal and self-reported. If a person
experiences persistent sexual or mood symptoms, the appropriate step is to speak with a
healthcare professional, not to self-attribute the symptoms to a "flatline" phase.
