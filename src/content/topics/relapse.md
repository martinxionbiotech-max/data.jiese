## What relapse is

Relapse is the return to a problematic behaviour after a period of abstinence or reduced
use. Recovery communities generally frame relapse not as failure but as a common part of
the change process.

## What is known

In addiction research generally, lapses are common and recovery is non-linear. For PPU
specifically, relapse is described extensively in community reports but has been less
studied in controlled research. Community members emphasize that recovering quickly from
a lapse matters more than never lapsing.

## Practical angle

Planning for setbacks in advance — deciding ahead of time what to do after a lapse — is
generally more useful than assuming lapses will not happen. A lapse does not erase prior
progress. See [self-regulation](/topics/self-regulation/) and
[behaviour change](/topics/impulse-control/).
