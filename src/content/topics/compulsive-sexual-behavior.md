## What compulsive sexual behaviour is

Compulsive sexual behaviour describes sexual behaviour a person feels driven to perform
repeatedly, often despite negative consequences, and that feels hard to control. It is a
descriptive term, used across clinical and research settings.

## How it is understood

The ICD-11 recognizes [CSBD](/topics/csbd/); the DSM-5 does not. Research such as
[Bőthe et al. (2019)](/research/bothe-2019-impulsivity/) suggests compulsivity is a more
important feature than impulsivity. Skeptical work such as
[Walton et al. (2017)](/research/walton-2017-hypersexuality/) questions whether the
construct is valid at all.

## Practical relevance

If the pattern is compulsive, interrupting patterns may matter more than resisting single
urges. The term itself does not imply a diagnosis — what matters clinically is distress
and impairment. See the [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).
