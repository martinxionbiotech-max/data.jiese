## What stress is

Stress is the mind's and body's response to demands or threats. People under stress often
turn to coping behaviours, and when pornography becomes the primary coping mechanism,
that use can become problematic.

## Stress as a trigger

Stress is among the most commonly reported triggers for pornography use and relapse in
community reports, and stress reliably increases the risk of relapse across many
problematic behaviours. The supporting evidence for PPU specifically is mostly
self-report, not controlled trials.

## Practical angle

Because stress is such a common antecedent, building non-pornography coping strategies —
exercise, social contact, adequate sleep, relaxation — is a practical way to reduce the
frequency of urges. See [emotion regulation](/topics/emotion-regulation/).
