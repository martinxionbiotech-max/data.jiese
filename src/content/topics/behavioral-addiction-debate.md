## The central question

The behavioural addiction debate asks whether compulsive sexual behaviour and problematic
pornography use should be classified and treated as addictions. **The scientific
classification remains debated** — this is not a settled matter.

## The two sides

- **The addiction model** points to parallels with substance and gambling addiction:
  cue reactivity, craving, loss of control, and continued use despite harm (see
  [Kraus et al. (2016)](/research/kraus-2016-should-csb-addiction/)).
- **The impulse-control / compulsion frameworks** and skeptical views point to the role of
  [moral incongruence](/research/grubbs-2019-moral-incongruence/), the absence of a
  validated withdrawal syndrome, and methodological weaknesses in the literature (see
  [de Alarcón et al. (2019)](/research/de-alarcon-2019-systematic-review/)).

## Why this matters

Community culture strongly favours the addiction framing, but that is a lens, not a
scientific conclusion. Understanding the debate helps people avoid over-pathologizing
while still taking their distress seriously.
