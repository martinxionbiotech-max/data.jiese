## What "withdrawal" means

In addiction frameworks, withdrawal describes distress or symptoms when a behaviour or
substance is stopped or reduced. Whether this applies to pornography is an open question.

## The evidence problem

Some measurement instruments — for example, the [PPCS](/research/bothe-2018-ppcs/) —
include "withdrawal" as a component of problematic use. But these instruments assume an
addiction framework in their design. There is no established, measurable withdrawal
syndrome for pornography comparable to substance withdrawal.

## What people actually report

Community members commonly describe withdrawal-like experiences when quitting —
irritability, restlessness, strong urges, low mood — and usually describe them as
temporary. These reports are real as experiences but do not, by themselves, establish a
physiological withdrawal syndrome. See also the [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).
