## What CSBD is

Compulsive Sexual Behaviour Disorder (CSBD) is the ICD-11 diagnosis for a persistent
pattern of failing to control intense, repetitive sexual impulses or urges, where the
behaviour causes marked distress or significant impairment. It is classified under
impulse control disorders and took effect in 2022.

## Divergence between classification systems

The ICD-11 includes CSBD, but the DSM-5 does not include a comparable diagnosis — it
rejected a proposed "hypersexual disorder." This divergence is a concrete sign that the
scientific classification [remains debated](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).

## Community vs. clinical terms

Recovery communities rarely use "CSBD"; they speak of "porn addiction" or "sex
addiction." Those community terms are not formal diagnoses. Understanding the difference
is part of this knowledge base's purpose. See the
[CSBD definition](/topics/csbd-icd11/) for more.
