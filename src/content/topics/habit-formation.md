## What a habit is

A habit is a behaviour that has become automatic through repetition and is triggered by
cues in the environment or situation rather than by deliberate intention. Habit
researchers distinguish goal-directed behaviour (done for an expected outcome) from
habitual behaviour (triggered by cues, often regardless of the outcome).

## How this applies to pornography use

Many people describe pornography use as "automatic" — it happens in familiar situations,
such as late at night, alone, or when stressed, almost without a decision. That is
consistent with a strong habit: the cue triggers the behaviour before deliberation.

## Practical angle

Because habits are cue-driven, a common approach is to change the cues and routines
around use — the environment, timing, and device placement — and to build competing
behaviours. Whether the behaviour is better called a "habit," an "addiction," or a
"compulsion" is part of the broader [behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/).
