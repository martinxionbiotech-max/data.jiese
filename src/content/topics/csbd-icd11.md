## What this term means

Compulsive Sexual Behaviour Disorder (CSBD) is the ICD-11's formal diagnosis for a
persistent pattern of failing to control intense, repetitive sexual impulses or urges,
where the resulting behaviour causes marked distress or significant impairment. It is
classified under **impulse control disorders**, not under substance-related or addictive
disorders.

## Why the placement matters

The ICD-11 deliberately does not call CSBD a "sexual addiction." This is a meaningful
choice: it signals that the World Health Organization did not conclude that the available
evidence supports an addiction classification. See the
[behavioural addiction debate](/topics/behavioral-addiction-debate/) for the full picture.

## Community vs. clinical language

Recovery communities frequently use the words "porn addiction" or "sex addiction." These
are community terms, not ICD-11 or DSM-5 diagnoses. They may describe a person's felt
experience, but they do not carry a formal clinical definition.
