## What emotion regulation is

Emotion regulation refers to the processes by which people influence which emotions they
have, when they have them, and how they experience and express them. Difficulties here
are linked to many problematic behaviours.

## How it relates to pornography use

Community members very commonly describe pornography use as a way to cope with stress,
boredom, anxiety, loneliness, or low mood — as an escape rather than a source of
pleasure. Theoretical accounts such as the [I-PACE model](/research/brand-2016-ipace/)
place poor emotion regulation at the centre of how problematic use develops.

## Practical angle

A recurring recovery theme is building alternative ways to regulate emotion: noticing
feelings, and developing other coping strategies so that pornography is not the default
response to distress. See also [stress](/topics/stress/) and [boredom](/topics/boredom/).
