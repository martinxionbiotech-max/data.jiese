## What "PIED" is

"Porn-induced erectile dysfunction" (PIED) is a term used in recovery communities and
some clinical reports for erectile dysfunction attributed to pornography use. It is
**not** a formally recognized medical diagnosis, and its causal link to pornography is
**not established**.

## What the evidence shows

On one side, clinical reports — reviewed in
[Park et al. (2016)](/research/park-2016-pied/) — describe young men presenting with
erectile dysfunction attributed to pornography. On the other side, general-population
studies such as [Landripet & Štulhofer (2015)](/research/landripet-2015/) and reviews such
as [Prause & Pfaus (2015)](/research/prause-pfaus-2015/) find little consistent
association. The causal claim remains unproven.

## Practical implications

Erectile dysfunction has many causes — psychological, relational, medical, and
lifestyle-related. It should be evaluated by a healthcare professional rather than
self-attributed to pornography. Reducing pornography use may help some people, but it is
not a guaranteed treatment.
