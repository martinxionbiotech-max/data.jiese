## What cue reactivity is

Cue reactivity is the tendency for a cue — a time, place, device, image, or feeling — to
trigger a conditioned response such as craving or the urge to act. It is one of the most
well-established concepts in addiction research.

## How it shows up in PPU

Community members reliably describe specific cues preceding their urges, and identifying
these cues is frequently reported as a turning point. On the research side,
[Voon et al. (2014)](/research/voon-2014-cue-reactivity/) and
[Gola et al. (2017)](/research/gola-2017-fmri/) found heightened neural reactivity to
sexual cues in some groups, though the studies are small and mixed (see also
[Prause et al. (2015)](/research/prause-2015-lpp/)).

## Practical angle

Mapping personal cues is a concrete, low-risk starting point: what situations or feelings
reliably precede urges? Reducing exposure to high-risk cues is a common harm-reduction
strategy.
